# 💰 Orlevy Finance

**Orlevy Finance** is a modern finance web application designed to help users **track expenses, manage budgets, and visualize financial data** in an intuitive and interactive way.  

🌐 Check it out here: [Orlevy Finance Website](https://orlevy-finance.vercel.app/)  
💻 GitHub Repository: [Orlevy Finance on GitHub](https://github.com/DolevAtik/orlevy-finance)

---

## ✨ Features
- 📊 **Expense Tracking** – Keep track of all your expenses easily.  
- 💵 **Budget Management** – Set and manage budgets for different categories.  
- 📈 **Data Visualization** – View your financial data with clear charts and graphs.  
- 🔒 **Secure & Private** – Your data stays safe on your account.  
- 🖥️ **Responsive Design** – Works smoothly on desktop and mobile devices.  

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS, Chart.js  
- **Backend & Hosting:** Vercel  
- **Database/Storage:** LocalStorage / Client-side storage  

---

## 📌 Why Orlevy Finance?
Orlevy Finance is perfect for anyone looking to **take control of their finances**, make smarter spending decisions, and track progress over time. Its clean design and interactive charts make managing money simple and even enjoyable!
